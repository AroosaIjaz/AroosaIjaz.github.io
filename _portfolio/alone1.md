<br/>

**A passing thought from the mind of an eighty-year old homeless man on the streets of Toronto:**

<br/>

My beard grows ever longer and greyer

The night grows ever colder and longer

The void grows ever darker and closer

<br/>
<br/>

I want to feel numb

<br/>
<br/>

As I sit here, under the starry night

invisible and forgotten 

fighting and existing 

moment to moment

<br/>
<br/>

I want to feel numb

<br/>
<br/>


The warmth of a human touch feels like a distant memory now

yet it is what I desire the most....

to be seen 

to be told I am seen

to be loved

to be told I am loved

to be tucked into bed and kissed on the forehead

<br/>
<br/>

I want to feel numb

<br/>
<br/>

I am no longer bothered by the sounds and smells , by the heat of these clothes, by the pinching of these shoes, by the pain in my back, by the feet rushing past me, by the memories of old promises, by the masked tricks of this life, and by the emptiness of everything...

<br/>
<br/>

I want to feel numb

<br/>
<br/>


The other end of this stream of consciousness is a bottomless fall into despair 

<br/>
<br/>

I want to feel numb

<br/>
<br/>

Yet

my inextinguishable human need 

breaks through into my consciousness

bringing forth with it

all of my humanity

<br/>
<br/>

I want to feel numb

<br/>
<br/>

Can anyone see me?
